# HeJiaVillage Unity Feature References

The `*Reference.png` images are manual planning references, not final terrain textures. They do not modify Terrain, colliders, NavMesh, scenes, spawning, networking, or saves. `HeJiaVillage_SurfaceControl.png` is the exception: it is a linear, readable data texture generated by `GenerateSurfaceControl.py` and drives TerrainLayer weights through the terrain profile. Pixel column increases east; image row increases south. Unity origin is the southwest corner of tile `1_1`; `x=column+0.5`, `z=2047.5-row`.

Slope material weights are sampled with a 30 m radius to match the nominal Copernicus GLO-30 source scale. The 20° start and 37° full thresholds follow the measured HeJiaVillage DEM distribution; they change only TerrainLayer weights and never alter height geometry.

Road widths are visibility symbols, not surveyed widths. The full-map road correction records only centerlines visibly confirmed on the 2026-09-22 Gaode standard-map view. The surface correction records only conservative interiors of ponds clearly visible on the satellite layer; ambiguous ditches, streams, buildings and land-cover boundaries are omitted. Neither correction stores provider raster or vector tiles. WorldCover built-up areas do not imply individual buildings. Missing OpenStreetMap features do not imply that a real-world feature is absent. Single-source records and boundary disagreements require manual judgement.
